# Changelog

What changed in the wiki, and when. Newest first, one line per change, naming the
pages it touched — this is the log that says whether a page was revisited after the
thing it describes moved.

- 2026-09-17 — Posing before animating. [[pixellab-asset-routing]] gains `enhance-animation-v3-prompt` and says what `custom_start_frame` and `end_frame` do to an animation call; PixelLab's advice is to animate from a posed state rather than from the neutral rotation, and a mid-walk state animated here confirmed it. The website's own `animate-with-text-v3/character/background` and `enhance-animation-prompt` endpoints stay out of scope under `adr:0002-call-pixellab-rest-v2-directly`; every capability exists in v2 under documented names.
- 2026-09-15 — The four-direction character route. [[pixellab-asset-routing]] gains `create-character-with-4-directions`, which is a separate template-based model rather than v3 asked for fewer views, and [[pixellab-cost-model]] puts it in the base tier. The web editor's *AI Freedom* and *Template Padding* controls are deliberately absent: neither exists in the public schema, and `adr:0002-call-pixellab-rest-v2-directly` puts the website's own endpoints out of scope.
- 2026-09-15 — Added [[harness-instructions]], from each harness's own documentation: Claude Code's skill directory, Codex's `AGENTS.md` scoping and its `$CODEX_HOME` preference for `AGENTS.override.md`, opencode's `instructions` array. Written because `pixellab setup` has to know all three and none of it is in their READMEs.
- 2026-09-15 — Four routes the table did not carry, and the anchor. [[pixellab-asset-routing]] gains `generate-with-style-v2`, `create-character-state`, `transfer-outfit-v2` and `animate-pixminimax`; [[pixellab-cost-model]] gains the first three in the Pro Tools tier and says why the fourth cannot have one; [[concept-to-sprite]] renames its first step the anchor and says what every route downstream assumes about it.
- 2026-09-14 — Added [[the-command-surface]], written after the CLI existed rather than before: what the commands are, what they share, and which decisions live in which module.
- 2026-09-14 — First pass. Distilled `https://api.pixellab.ai/v2/llms.txt`, the v2 OpenAPI document, fal's Python client documentation, the GPT Image 2.5 queue schemas, and the PixelLab research under `ref/pixellab-pip/docs/pixellab/` into [[pixellab-api]], [[pixellab-asset-routing]], [[pixellab-terminology]], [[pixellab-style-controls]], [[pixellab-cost-model]], [[fal-platform]], [[gpt-image-25]], [[concept-to-sprite]] and [[generation-record]].
